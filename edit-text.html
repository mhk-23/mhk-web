Edit page text

Write the text for any page of your site here. Nothing to code.

  1. Pick a page from the list.
  2. Type or paste its text. A blank line starts a new paragraph. Web addresses and email addresses turn into links by themselves.
  3. Click Save text file. Move the file it downloads, site-text.js, into your site's content folder and replace the old one.
  4. Reload your site (Ctrl+Shift+R). You can come back here any time and your text will still be here.

Contact page links

Paste the link to your profile on each platform. Clicking that name on the Contact page opens it. Leave one empty to switch that link off.

Your email and phone number are already set up. They appear in their own boxes when the mouse is over Email or Phone.
